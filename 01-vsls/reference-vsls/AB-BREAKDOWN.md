# AB Nasdaq's VSL, frame by frame

Everything here was measured on `abnasdaqvsl.mp4` (scripts: `04-edit/scripts/ab_zoom_analysis.py`,
`ab_caption_analysis.py`), then copied for Ricky's variant B2 (`04-edit/scripts/plan_ab2.py`).

## Captions (bottom)

| What | AB | Ricky B2 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Words per caption | 2-3 ("that I unfortunately", "cannot show you", "because they restricted", "access to it") | 1-2 (as asked) |
| How they change | Hard switch: the next caption replaces the last on the very next frame, no fade, no pop | Same |
| Font | Tight Apple-style sans (SF Pro Display look), medium weight, about 90 px, very tight letter spacing | Inter Display Medium 92 px, tracking −3.5 |
| Case | Lowercase, no punctuation; "I" and names keep their capitals | Same |
| Place | Centred, around y 885 of 1080 | Same |
| Readability | Soft dark shadow + a dark fade over the bottom ~40% of the frame (his bright window goes from 183 to 107 brightness toward the bottom edge) | Same fade (black, 55% at the bottom edge) and a soft shadow |

## Side text (his signature)

- Key lines move off the bottom and stack up in the empty side of the frame: a regular line, then **HEAVY CAPS**,
  with the key word in electric blue ("Every one / OF THESE / **VIDEOS**", "starts off / **THE SAME** / exact way",
  "The Syndicate runs / **for 90 days**").
- The side gets a **white fade from that edge** (strong at the edge, gone by about the middle) and the text is black on
  it; on darker backgrounds the fade is grey and the text white.
- It builds as he says it: each line appears on its word, letter by letter with a quick blur-in.
- The word captions at the bottom disappear while side text is up.
- B2 uses this for ~30 key lines through the whole video, left or right, whichever side is empty.

## Sound effects

- 133 effect sounds in 6:13, about one every 2.8 s.
- Most are **short, crisp clicks/ticks** (30-80 ms, bright, 3-8 kHz): every piece of side text and most graphics land
  on one (in the hook: 0.4, 1.9, 3.1, 3.6, 4.2, 4.8 s, one per text piece).
- A few longer **whooshes** (0.15-0.4 s, darker) on bigger transitions.
- His bottom word captions themselves don't carry a click (the high-frequency level at caption changes is the same as
  at random moments). B2 adds a very soft click on every caption anyway, as asked, a few dB under the clicks on side text.

## Zoom and cutting

- A cut every ~4 s between his face, graphics and proof.
- On his face the shot is almost never still: about 80% of face shots slowly push in or pull out by 15-25% over
  5-15 s, alternating. B2 does the same (never faster than 5% per second), with only a small drift while side text
  needs the empty side.

## Graphics

- White keynote cards with black words appearing one by one; big blue numbers on white; proof as clean cards on a light
  grey plate; black cards pairing a photo or phone with mixed-weight text ("his name is / JAMES"); a list with an arrow on
  the current item; a split screen for the offer ("WHAT'S INCLUDED INSIDE THE SYNDICATE") building line by line; giant
  text behind his head ("ONE SETUP").
- Things B2 can't copy without new material: his 3D money and objects, movie clips and stock footage.

## Music

- A soft bed under the talking (quieter than Iman's, about 17 dB under his voice in B2), a track with a beat over the
  proof montage.
