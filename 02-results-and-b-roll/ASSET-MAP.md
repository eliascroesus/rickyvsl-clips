## Before using any of these

- **Everything is vertical** (phone photos, screenshots and clips). The VSL is 16:9, so each one goes in a
  frame or phone mockup on a branded background, the way Iman Gadzhi and AB show their screens. Nothing gets
  stretched or shown raw full-screen.
- **Store name.** It's scribbled out in red on `IMG_0037` and `IMG_0039`, but readable as "Bondle Cam" in the
  browser tab and URL on `IMG_8623`. Decide whether it should be hidden; if so it gets blurred everywhere.
  The red scribbles look messy on screen, so those two are better cropped to the numbers or rebuilt as clean
  number cards.
- **Chargebacks are visible** on `IMG_0028` (22) and `IMG_0045.PNG` (8). Crop or blur them out.
- **Shipping labels** on `IMG_3292` are mostly blacked out; check nothing personal is readable before using it
  big.
- **`IMG_0038`, `IMG_0042` and `IMG_0043`** are three different September recaps (91M, 44.1M and 60.4M views).
  They look like three different channels. Confirm with Ricky before adding them up (that would be ~195.6M
  views in one month).
