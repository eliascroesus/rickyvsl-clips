# FAQ videos: raw footage

Ricky's raw FAQ recordings go here, one video per question, to be edited.

- **The videos themselves (up to 2 GB each):** use the
  [FAQ raw footage release](https://github.com/eliascroesus/rickyvsl-clips/releases/new?tag=faq-raw-footage&title=FAQ%20raw%20footage)
  (if GitHub asks about the tag, pick **Create new tag … on publish**). Drag all the videos into the box at the
  bottom (several at once is fine), wait until every one has finished uploading, then click **Publish release**.
- **More videos later:** open the [FAQ raw footage release](https://github.com/eliascroesus/rickyvsl-clips/releases/tag/faq-raw-footage),
  click the pencil (**Edit**), drag the new videos in and click **Update release**.
- **Small files (under 25 MB)**, e.g. the list of questions, a script, or pictures to show in them:
  [upload to this folder](https://github.com/eliascroesus/rickyvsl-clips/upload/claude/nice-rubin-bayjpu/faq-raw-footage).

Naming each file after its question (e.g. `faq-how-long-until-first-sale.mp4`) helps, but isn't required.
